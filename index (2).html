About Me

Hi, I'm Henrick!

I'm 18 years old and a first-year college student taking Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) at Far Eastern University.

I'm still new to college life, and every day I'm learning something different, from technology to time management to handling school on my own. I'm a student who is curious, hardworking and always trying to improve.

I'm more of an extrovert. I enjoy talking to people, meeting new classmates and sharing ideas. Being around others gives me energy, and I believe good teamwork starts with good communication.

Something people might not expect: even though I love playing games and being active, I also enjoy sitting down with a good book. I can be energetic and relaxed at the same time, and I like that balance.

Hobbies and Interests

Playing games and sports has been my hobby since I was little. Whether it's running around with friends or playing on my phone, it has always been my way to have fun, relax and get rid of stress.

These days, I mostly play games on my phone and read whenever I have free time. Games teach me strategy and teamwork, while reading helps me learn new things and imagine new ideas.

Things That Keep Me Curious

  • How game characters and logos are designed.
  • How color, font and layout make a poster stand out.
  • How AI and apps are changing the way we work.
  • How design, IT and engineering can work together.

What's on My Playlist

My favorite song is Always by Daniel Caesar. It's calm and soothing, and I listen to it whenever I want to relax or focus.

Goals and Aspirations

My dream is to become a graphic designer and an industrial engineer. I want to combine creativity and technical skills to create designs that are useful, meaningful and good-looking.

What I'm Working Toward

  • Doing well in my studies and keeping good grades.
  • Learning the basics of IT, programming and networking.
  • Practicing graphic design skills at home.
  • Building good study habits and managing my time.
  • Finishing my school tasks on time while still resting.

Right now, I'm a student who works and studies from home. It isn't always easy to stay focused, but I'm learning discipline one day at a time.

My biggest dream is to become a successful graphic designer and to be successful in life. I want to build a good future for myself and make my family proud of what I achieve.

It's only the beginning, but I'm ready to learn, grow and keep going.
